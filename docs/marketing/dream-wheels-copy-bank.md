## Suggested use

- Use “Примерьте. Сравните. Выберите.” as the short hero or section headline.
- Use “Visual wheel try-on powered by AI” as a compact supporting descriptor when an English product line fits the context.
- Use “15 вариантов для BMW 3 Series” as catalog context or a configurator section label; replace the vehicle and count with current data when used in-product.

## Tone reference

The intended direction is “умный премиум без агрессии”: light, calm and sophisticated, with large visual storytelling and minimal interface chrome. The catalog should feel closer to a premium configurator than to a conventional online shop.

Keep the language short and confident. Treat the Dream Wheels acid-green accent (`#ddff00`) as a restrained highlight rather than part of the copy itself.
